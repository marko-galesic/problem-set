Implement a function topKFrequent that returns the k most frequent words in a list.

Method Signature

public String[] topKFrequent(String[] words, int k)

Requirements

  • Return the k most frequent words from words
  • Order results by descending frequency
  • Break ties by lexicographic order ascending
  • All returned words must be distinct

Constraints

  • 1 <= words.length <= 100000
  • 1 <= words[i].length <= 30
  • Each word contains lowercase English letters
  • 1 <= k <= number of unique words in words

Example

Input:

words = ["i", "love", "leetcode", "i", "love", "coding"]
k = 2

Output:

["i", "love"]

Explanation: Both i and love appear twice, and i comes first lexicographically.

Additional Example

Input: words = ["the", "day", "is", "sunny", "the", "the", "the", "sunny", "is", "is"], k = 4
Output: ["the", "is", "sunny", "day"]
